Capstone Thumb disassembly confirms both font-manager calls still target the
stock builder, with unchanged preimages `ff f7 36 fb` and `ff f7 07 fb`.
Their surrounding code passes four font configurations, stores the returned
chain and checks it for NULL. There are exactly two direct BL matches to the
builder. The five direct letter-helper BL matches are `0x00492D08`,
`0x00492D90`, `0x00493772`, `0x004A89B8`, and `0x004A8C22`.

At `0x00492EE0`, the stock helper's PC-relative load resolves to `0x00493244`.
That literal contains `0x007BD720`, whose word is decoder Thumb entry
`0x00492FF7`. The wrapper follows both indirections. The decoder explicitly
substitutes its stack slot for a NULL offset, preserving lookahead behavior.
The artifact emulation tests exercise ASCII, Thai and NULL-offset decoding
through this real new stock decoder.

The allocator retains its size argument, mutex acquire/release and returned
allocation behavior. Its stock allocation delegate is now `0x0049C946`.
The glyph-descriptor routine still clears `0x20` bytes and follows fallback
word `0x1C`; glyph release still reads cache-entry word `0x1C` and the callback
at font offset 8. The IAR descriptor ABI and bounded SRAM cache remain intact.

## Companion and verification

The vendored companion stays at `c437fdf22b71c7e3c0b9de2b4669d8a4847ce919`.
Its local patch is regenerated with **both** the Thai artifact and matching
stock rollback bundle pinned independently of the hosted catalog. This keeps
our custom Case-USB workflow without importing SybilSight's CFW removal.
Stock rollback is classified as official; Thai is classified as local custom.
Both require exact whole-file/main hashes. Thai cannot stage Case firmware;
both local-only targets remain blocked from direct BLE transfer.

Offline checks:

- `make check`: 48 tests, no skips, including new mapping, anchor uniqueness,
  component preservation, corrupted-stock rejection and existing emulation.
- Independent pinned `g2flash.validate_firmware`: both stock and patched
  bundles pass six-component validation and Apollo bounds/checksums.
- `make webflasher`: exact bundle and main pin match.
- `node tools/check_webflasher_artifacts.mjs`: both real files accepted by
  parser/final writer gate; altered payloads and unknown bundle pins rejected.
- Companion Node suite: 345 tests, no skips; Vite production build passes.
- Companion Python suite: 25 tests pass with its declared pyserial dependency.
- Local patch applies cleanly to a fresh checkout of the pinned companion.
- Browser at `http://127.0.0.1:3000/#firmware`: both actual local files show
  **Validated locally**, the correct 2.2.10.10 labels and hashes, with no console
  errors. No Case or temple was connected during validation.
- `autoreview --mode local --codex-bin /Applications/ChatGPT.app/Contents/Resources/codex`:
  clean, no accepted/actionable findings. The reviewer independently checked
  binaries, relocation anchors, component preservation and both writer pins.

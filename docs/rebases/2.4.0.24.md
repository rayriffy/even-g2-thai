preparing a local Case-USB transfer. Hardware validation remains pending.

## Container mapping and stock dependencies

The main component starts at OTA offset `0x000BEF35`; its payload starts at
`0x000BEFB5` and contains 3,700,100 bytes. Preamble byte 32 maps to installed
MRAM address `0x00438000`, so `file_offset = address - 0x0037902B`.
The odd file offset is valid: instruction alignment is in installed MRAM.
The stock main ends at `0x007BF564`.

These regions moved independently. Unique stock byte anchors of at least
16 bytes, plus decoded Thumb control flow and literal references, authenticate
the roles. Both font calls still build four-entry foreground/background
chains; the only direct `BL` callers of `0x004758F0` are the two patched sites.
Their preimages are now `fff774fb` and `fff745fb`.

The letter helper's load at `0x0049F1F0` resolves to literal `0x0049F554`,
containing storage address `0x007B9ED0`, whose word is decoder Thumb entry
`0x0049F307`. The wrapper preserves both indirections and executes the real
stock decoder under emulation, including NULL-offset lookahead.

Glyph lookup retains the 32-byte IAR descriptor and fallback word 7; release
retains cache-entry word `0x1C`. The lookup trampoline replays the exact
`PUSH.W {r1-r11, lr}` and resumes at `0x00508B5D` with the Thumb bit set.
Allocator and XIP guard addresses are updated in the emulation fixtures too.

## Authenticated install boundary

The stock flag read at `0x0044AC68` loads literal `0x0044B210`, containing
`0x007FE000`, then dereferences and compares the flag magic. The OTA writer
at `0x00483708` independently loads that destination from `0x00483848`.
Settings write at `0x00483666` loads destination `0x007FF000` from
`0x00483830`. These observations authenticate `0x007FE000` as this version's
patch ceiling. They do not establish a boundary for another firmware.

## Verification and companion

`make check` exercises the final decoder, lookup trampoline, native fallback,
glyph metrics/bitmaps, buffers, cache paths and Thai attachment references.
Rebase regressions verify the odd container mapping, unique anchors, both
chain callers, decoder indirections, component identity, and rejection of
a checksummed image beyond the authenticated flag.

Stock and candidate pass `g2flash.validate_firmware` from pinned commit
`877c8d9490db0d3717ca012dd0f54556af3701bd`, including its original conservative
`0x007F0000` limit without any override. Both also pass this repository's
component CRC32C and main preamble CRC-32/load/length checks.

The companion remains at `c437fdf22b71c7e3c0b9de2b4669d8a4847ce919`.
Its portable patch pins the exact candidate and same-version stock rollback
bundle/main hashes. Both are local-only; direct BLE remains blocked.
Parser/writer mutation checks, companion tests/build, clean patch application
and archive preparation are recorded in `GATES.md`.

Physical Case-USB transfer, boot, display quality and rollback remain untested.
No device connection or write is part of this rebase.
